# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in PortHarbor a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing sensitive information.
- Help newcomers participate productively.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other appropriate corrective action.
